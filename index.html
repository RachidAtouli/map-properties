About Meเกี่ยวกับฉัน

Your Local Guide to Bangkok Rentalsไกด์ท้องถิ่นของคุณสำหรับการเช่าในกรุงเทพฯ

I am Supatchaya Thummachat, an independent rental consultant based in Bangkok. After years of helping expat friends navigate the frustrations of dealing with landlords, language barriers, and confusing processes, I decided to turn that experience into a service.

Finding the right home in a new city can feel overwhelming, especially when you are unfamiliar with neighborhoods, landlord expectations, or local practices. That is where I come in.

With deep knowledge of Bangkok's rental landscape and a network built on trust, I act as your cultural bridge: translating not just language, but expectations. I focus exclusively on long term rentals, ensuring you find a place that truly fits your lifestyle and budget.
